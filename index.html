Popularity of Halloween Candies Throughout the Years

Are these candies a trick, or a real treat?

What's your favorite Halloween Candy? Over the years, Halloween has been a marker for which treats reign supreme. Kids everywhere go house to house trick or treating and are met with great joy or sorrow depending on what is added to their baggies. For the longest time, Candy corn was seen as the epitome of a Halloween delicacy. Nowadays, it serves more as a themed decoration than anything anyone would actually consume.

In recent years, the candy assortment has become much more artificial than it had been in years prior. Whereas candies back in the day were mostly some assortment of coated nuts, today we see shelves filled with highly processed sweets containing artificial flavors and preservatives. While these additions may make candy more appealing to consumers, they also raise questions about what exactly we're putting into our bodies.
